# AgileCert Programme Catalogue Foundation

## Purpose

Provide candidates with a read-only preview of proposed AgileCert certification pathways before any examination, question-bank, pricing or enrolment activation.

## Included

- Candidate navigation launcher labelled `Programmes`.
- Three catalogue categories:
  - Agile Project Management, Product and Delivery Certifications
  - Agile Human Resource Management Certifications
  - Agile Leadership Certifications
- Foundation, Associate, Professional, Specialist and Executive level badges.
- Search, category and level filters.
- 38 proposed programme codes, names and summaries extracted from the Google AI Studio workspace.
- Clear preview and academic-review notices.

## Explicitly excluded

- Supabase migrations or production data writes.
- Examination creation or publishing.
- Question-bank or answer-key deployment.
- Examination fees, certification fees or currency conversion.
- Checkout, order, payment or entitlement changes.
- Certificate templates, certificate rendering or credential issuance.
- Finance Console, coupon, refund, scholarship or reconciliation changes.
- Authentication, role, candidate profile or audit-control changes.
- GitHub Actions, Namecheap, GitHub Pages or Supabase deployment workflow changes.

## Release boundary

This package is safe to review independently because it is frontend-only and read-only. It preserves every existing production service and introduces no candidate purchase or examination-launch path.

## Later phases

1. Academic approval of programme names and curricula.
2. Question-bank quality review and protected Supabase seeding.
3. Finance approval of separate NGN and USD prices.
4. Pilot examination activation through a separate production release.
